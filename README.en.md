## Projects

### [My study tests](https://claude.ai/artifact/Uuz8SHFxgoL6TdPPrL6yZh)

I upload my notes for each subject (PDF, Word or photos) and the app turns them into exam-style tests. It keeps track of what I get wrong in each topic, so I know what to go over again. I use it for Computer Systems, Databases and Development Environments. *(The app is in Spanish.)*

<sub>Built with the help of AI</sub>

### [Agendado](https://agendado-kappa.vercel.app)

A website where hair salons, barbershops and tattoo studios run their schedule and customers book online. The business sets up its hours, services and staff, and customers only see the slots that are actually free. Under the hood, the database stops two people from booking the same time even if they hit the button at once. It has Stripe payments and a QR code the business can print and stick on the shop window. *(The site is in Spanish.)*

`Next.js` · `React` · `TypeScript` · `PostgreSQL` · `Prisma` · `Tailwind CSS` · `Stripe`

<sub>Built with the help of AI</sub>
